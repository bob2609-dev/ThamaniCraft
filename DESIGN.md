# ThamaniCraft Design Specification

## Visual Philosophy
Artisanal Craft meets High-Precision Manufacturing. High contrast, large tactile touch targets for kitchen environments with flour/glove interactions, paired with high-density tabular financial data in the back-office.

## Typography
- Base UI: `Inter`
- SKU Codes, Batch Numbers, Recipe Quantities & Currency: `JetBrains Mono` / `monospace`
- Currency Standard: `TZS 1,234,567` (strictly formatted)\n
